---
title: Pricing & credits
description: One plan at $1 a month, credits at $1 each or less in bulk, and exactly what each scan costs.
---

Sandchest has one plan: $1 a month, and credits at $1 each, less when you buy more at once. Paid scans draw from the account's credits, and most pull request reviews cost 1. There are no per-seat prices and no repository limits: credits are the only limit.

## Plans

| Plan | Per month | Credits | Per credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $1 | Bought as you need them | $1, down to $0.80 in bulk |

- Credits are bought ahead and never expire. Buy them when you join, from **Settings → Billing** at any time, or let auto top-up buy them when you run low.
- The customer is the GitHub account Sandchest is installed on, not a person. Everyone with access to that account's repositories shares its credits.
- Prices are in US dollars. Stripe may show the amount in your local currency at checkout.
- Accounts on an earlier plan (Lockbox, Chest or Vault) keep it, and its monthly credits, until they switch.

## What scans cost

| Scan | When it runs | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recon | When a repository is added | Free |
| PR review | A pull request is opened or updated | 1 to 3, by size |
| Deep scan | When you press **Deep scan** | 5, 10 or 25, by repository size |
| Fix check | Pushes to the default branch | Free |

Recon and fix checks are free, but they still need the account to be on the plan.

### PR reviews

A review costs credits by the number of security-relevant lines the pull request changes: lines added plus lines removed, not counting lockfiles, generated or vendored code, docs, tests and assets. Most pull requests are 1 credit.

| Changed lines | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 500 | 1 |
| Up to 2,000 | 2 |
| Over 2,000 | 3 |

Above 5,000 changed lines the review covers the most security-relevant files first (those with the most entry points and dangerous code) up to 5,000 lines. A pull request that only touches docs, tests, lockfiles or generated files costs nothing.

### Deep scans

Your account's first deep scan is free, whatever the repository's size: the **Deep scan** button says "Free: your first deep scan". If that scan fails, the next one is free instead.

After that, a deep scan costs credits by the repository's size in lines of code, not counting vendored or generated code. Recon measures it, and from then on the **Deep scan** button shows the price before you start. Before recon has measured a repository (about a minute after you install or join), the button says "Up to 25 credits": it holds 25, and charges the tier the scan measures. With fewer than 25 credits, the button waits for recon to size the repository and says so.

| Repository size | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Under 100,000 lines | 5 |
| Under 300,000 lines | 10 |
| Under 1,000,000 lines | 25 |
| 1,000,000 lines or more | Not scanned. Get in touch. |

Repositories of a million lines or more aren't scanned at all, by recon, reviews or deep scans.

## How credits are charged

- When a paid scan starts, its credits are held. When it finishes, it is charged what it cost, and never more than the price you were shown.
- If a scan fails, is refused or never starts, nothing is charged and the held credits go back to your balance.
- A pull request that arrives when the account is out of credits isn't reviewed. Its Check is **neutral** ("Out of credits"), so it never blocks a merge, and it links to where you can top up. The same happens, with "Not reviewed: no plan", before the account has a plan.
- When credits run out, the **Deep scan** button shows "Out of credits" instead of starting a scan.
- With auto top-up off, the account's admins get an email when credits run low (below 5) and when they run out. With it on, they get an email for each top-up, and one if a top-up fails.

## Buying credits

Credits are $1 each and never expire. Buy 10, 25, 50 or 100 when you join, and up to 1,000 at once from **Settings → Billing** after that. Buying more at once costs less per credit: 25 or more are $0.90 each, 50 or more $0.85, and 100 or more $0.80. The whole purchase is priced at the rate its size reaches, and auto top-ups get the same discount.

| Credits bought at once | Per credit | For example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 24 | $1.00 | 10 for $10 |
| 25 to 49 | $0.90 | 25 for $22.50, saving $2.50 |
| 50 to 99 | $0.85 | 50 for $42.50, saving $7.50 |
| 100 or more | $0.80 | 100 for $80, saving $20 |

### Auto top-up

Auto top-up buys credits for you when fewer than 5 are left, so pull requests keep getting reviewed. You choose how many it buys each time (the same amount you started with, unless you change it) and the most it may spend in a month: $50, or five top-ups if that is more, unless you change it. It's on when you join unless you untick it, and you can change or turn it off in Settings. Auto top-up charges your card directly, without a confirm page.

## Managing billing

Everything lives in [Settings](/dashboard/settings), under Billing. Only an admin of the GitHub organization, or the owner of a personal account, can change it.

- **Switching from an earlier plan.** Accounts on Lockbox, Chest or Vault can switch to Standard from Settings. Credits already bought stay.
- **Confirm before charging.** When a card is already on file, every credit purchase and plan change is shown on a confirm page, with the exact amount, before it is made. Auto top-up is the exception: it charges on its own, within your monthly limit.
- **Business invoices.** When you join, you can add your business's name, billing address and tax ID (a VAT number, EIN, ABN or the like, by country). Stripe keeps them and puts them on every invoice; Sandchest doesn't store them.
- **Card, invoices and cancelling** are handled in Stripe's customer portal, linked from Settings. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Payments are processed by Stripe, through the billing service Autumn. Sandchest never sees your card number.

## Self-hosted

A self-hosted Sandchest has billing turned off: no plan, no credits and nothing to buy. You pay only for your own servers and model usage. The operational limits still apply: the 24-hour wait between deep scans of a repository, no scans of repositories with a million lines or more, and at most two scans at a time per account, with two more for recon. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting#limits-without-billing).
