The keyboard sounds (laptop.flac, tactile.flac, thock.flac, clicky.flac,
typewriter.flac) are single keystrokes cut by scripts/keys/cut-keys.py from
these recordings on Freesound, each released into the public domain under
Creative Commons 0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The cut files are released under CC0 too.

- laptop.flac: a MacBook Pro keyboard, https://freesound.org/s/565645/
- tactile.flac: Cherry MX Brown switches, https://freesound.org/s/400167/
- thock.flac: a Rainy 75 keyboard, https://freesound.org/s/815614/
- clicky.flac: Cherry MX Blue switches, https://freesound.org/s/382057/
- typewriter.flac: an Olivetti Lettera 22 typewriter, https://freesound.org/s/123344/
